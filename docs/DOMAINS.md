# DOMAINS.md — the spiky-profile taxonomy

> Load-bearing taxonomy (ADR 0005). Every rule in every family skill is grouped under one of these domains. Adding or renaming a domain is an ADR-level change.

A **cognitive profile** is the shape of a person's abilities across these domains. Neurotypical profiles run flat (abilities cluster near the person's average); neurodivergent profiles run **spiky** — high peaks next to deep troughs. A skill in this family is one spiky profile expressed as output rules: it exists for the troughs, and it never fights the peaks.

**Normal vs expected** (the distinction that keeps this taxonomy non-deficit): *normal* is statistical — what most people's pattern looks like. *Expected* is normative — what a given age or role anticipates. They diverge constantly in spiky profiles: normal reasoning, below-expected processing speed; normal accuracy, below-expected speed under time pressure. The skills target that gap — they change the *demand*, not the person.

## The domains

| Domain | A peak looks like | A trough shows up as | Impact on agent output | Normal ≠ expected example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reasoning (verbal & nonverbal)** | Pattern recognition, systemizing, spatial or verbal strength | Reasoning hidden behind other bottlenecks (time, decoding) | The reader can handle the *logic*; never simplify the thinking, only the packaging | Average reasoning, below-expected timed-task performance |
| **Processing speed** | Fast routine handling of familiar material | Slow on timed or unfamiliar tasks despite strong reasoning | Give the point first, then detail at the reader's pace; no time pressure in wording | Normal accuracy, not-expected speed for grade/work level |
| **Working memory** | Detail retention in domains of interest | Holding 1–3 items at once; multi-step instructions collapse | One instruction at a time; nothing "kept in mind"; state restated on screen | Follows complex ideas, loses multi-step real-time demands |
| **Attention & inhibition** | Deep focus on high-interest work | Focus collapses on low-interest tasks; impulse control varies | One topic per reply; the next action obvious and small; distractions parked | Normal in preferred contexts, not across all contexts |
| **Executive function** | Strong planning/initiation when structured | Planning, prioritizing, task initiation, shifting lag | Numbered steps, explicit completion criteria, visible progress | Intellectually capable, not meeting organizational demands |
| **Language & communication** | Advanced vocabulary, precise expression | Word retrieval, fluency, or pragmatic use lags | Plain words, short clauses, literal phrasing, terms defined at first use | Normal test scores, not-expected pragmatic timing |
| **Memory (episodic, semantic, spatial)** | Exceptional factual/spatial recall in interest areas | Encoding or retrieval uneven across types | Key facts repeated where they're used, not where they were introduced | Outstanding recall in interests, average elsewhere |
| **Sensory processing** | — (mostly a tolerance variable) | Hyper/hypo-sensitivity to density, noise, motion | Low-density formatting; minimal decoration; calm tone | Normal cognition, not-expected endurance in dense content |
| **Social cognition** | Heightened empathy or system-based social analysis | Implicit cues and unwritten rules don't land | Say the context out loud; no reliance on what "everyone knows" | Reasons well about rules, misses intuitive timing |
| **Motor / praxis** | — (rarely touched by text output) | Coordination, handwriting, sequencing | Long copy-pasteable commands over fiddly manual entry | Strong ideas, not-expected motor follow-through |

Domains with no output rule in a given skill simply don't appear in that skill's Rules section.
