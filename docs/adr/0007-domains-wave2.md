# ADR 0007: DOMAINS.md gains Numeracy and Motivation & energy

**Status:** accepted 2026-08-24

## Context

ADR 0005 makes `docs/DOMAINS.md` load-bearing and adding a domain an ADR-level change. Wave 2 ships `i-have-dyscalculia` and `i-have-low-energy`, whose primary troughs have no row in the wave-1 taxonomy: number processing, and initiation/energy.

## Decision

Two domains join the taxonomy:

- **Numeracy** — quantity comparison, mental arithmetic, digit memory and transcription. The dyscalculia trough. Typical pairing: strong verbal or spatial reasoning (spiky), so the arithmetic is the hazard, never the logic.
- **Motivation & energy** — task initiation, reward sensitivity, energy budgeting. The depression/avolition trough. Distinct from Executive function: EF is *how* to structure work; Motivation & energy is *whether the start happens at all*.

## Consequences

- `DOMAINS.md` grows to 12 domains; wave-2 skills group their rules under the new rows.
- Existing skills are unaffected; none of their rules re-tag.
- Later waves (dyspraxia, sensory-only) know the process: new domain ⇒ new ADR.
