# SOURCES.md — master citation list

Rule-level `Source:` lines cite the short names below. Policy: ADR 0003 (strict trace; `synthesis` = fork's own judgment, never a fake citation). All accessed 2026-08-24.

## Standards & professional bodies

- **COGA** — W3C, *Cognitive and Learning Disabilities Accessibility (COGA): Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities*. Section-level citations (§3.x user stories, §4.x patterns). Grounds: working memory limits (§2.2), one-idea-per-block (§4.4.5), separated instructions (§4.4.9), white space (§4.4.10), clear/literal words (§4.4.1, §4.4.4), simple tense & voice, no nested clauses (§4.4.2–4.4.3), summaries (§4.4.8), breadcrumbs/state (§4.2.4), limiting interruptions (§4.6.1), short critical paths (§4.6.2), too much content (§4.6.3), no memorized/calculated values (§4.7.5), stated consequences (§4.8.3), cognitive stress / feeling safe (§3.7.4), task preparation (§4.6.4).
  https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/
- **BDA** — British Dyslexia Association, *Dyslexia Style Guide 2023* (PDF via BDA resources page). Grounds: readable sans-serif text, 12–14pt guidance, headings, left alignment, bold-over-italics emphasis, short sentences, bullet structure, cream/pastel background contrast.
  https://www.bdadyslexia.org.uk/resources
- **NAS-tips** — National Autistic Society, *Tips for effective communication with autistic pupils* (Joe Butler, 2022; adapted from *Is That Clear? Effective Communication in a Neurodiverse World*). Grounds: short instructions, one question at a time, limited options over open-ended asks, literal language, realistic timings, positive instruction (say what to do), same-words repetition.
  https://www.autism.org.uk/learn/knowledge-hub/professional-practice/communication-pupils
- **SPELL** — National Autistic Society, *The SPELL framework* (Structure, Positive approaches, Empathy, Low arousal, Links). Grounds: predictable structure, low-arousal presentation.
  https://www.autism.org.uk/learn/what-we-do/training/the-spell-framework
- **RCSLT** — Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists, *Long COVID: Brain fog and communication* factsheet (2023). Grounds: fluctuation over time, communication impact, management pacing.
  https://www.rcslt.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/RCSLT_Long-Covid_Communication-factsheet.pdf

## Academic literature

- **Cummings2023** — Cummings, *Long COVID: The impact on language and cognition*, PMC10286899. Grounds: brain-fog prevalence, language/word-retrieval and working-memory character.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10286899/
- **PMC11110614** — spiky ability profiles; relative strengths in reasoning alongside weaknesses in speed/executive function.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11110614/
- **Ramsay & Rostain** — *The Adult ADHD Tool Kit* (upstream's credited basis for `i-have-adhd`; unchanged).
